◎食材及佐料〔Ingredients〕：(5人份)​
▪️紅蘿蔔、鳳梨 ​ 各200克​
▪️西洋芹 ​ 50克​
▪️檸檬汁 ​ 約50cc​
▪️砂糖 ​ 60克​
▪️冰塊 ​ 100克​
▪️飲用水 ​ 700cc​
​
◎烹飪步驟：​
【刀工/前處理】​
1.紅蘿蔔洗淨去皮切薄片。​
2.鳳梨去皮，切2公分大丁。​
3.西洋芹用削皮刀去除粗纖維，切片備用。​
​
【調理作法】​
1.取一乾淨的果汁機，放入食材紅蘿蔔、鳳梨、西洋芹、水、冰塊，攪打至細緻無顆粒。​
2.加入糖、檸檬汁調味，攪打均勻即可享用。​
​
【特別說明】​
1.糖、檸檬汁可以依個人喜好增減。​
2.可以加少許蜂蜜提味。​
3.加少許冰塊一起攪打可以保持鮮度。​

◎Ingredients : （For 5 people）​
▪️Carrot、Pineapple ​ 200g​
▪️Celery ​ 50g​
▪️Lemon juice ​ 50cc​
▪️Sugar ​ 60g​
▪️Ice cube ​ 100g​
▪️Water ​ 700cc​
​
◎Instructions: ​
【Cutting/PRE-Ahead】​
1. Wash the carrots, peel and slice them.​
2. Peel the pineapple and dice it into 2 cm.​
3. Remove the tough strings that run down the back of each rib of celery, and slice it for later use​
​
【Directions】​
1. Use a blender and add in carrots, pineapples, celery, water, ice cubes, and process until fine and smooth . ​
2. Add sugar and lemon juice to taste, process slightly and enjoy.​
​
【Special notes】 ​
1. Sugar and lemon juice is adjusting according to personal preference. ​
2. Add honey to enhance the flavor if you prefer. ​
3. Ice cubes is for keep up the tastes of freshness. ​ ​
